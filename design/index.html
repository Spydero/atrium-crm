Recommendation for V1.0

Start with Cove as the shell (fastest, cleanest), then steal Signal’s lead drawer (chat + AI log) for the showcase moment. Atlas photo cards can come in once seed listings look good. That gives you a shippable pipeline first, without waiting for a full inbox product.

Proposed V1.0 scope (after you pick a look)

  1. Auth + sidebar shell
  2. Leads kanban (New → Qualified → Viewing → Offer → Won)
  3. Lead cards with property thumb, price, source badge
  4. KPI strip (leads, pipeline AED, viewings / reply time)
  5. Lead detail drawer stub (notes now; chat/AI next task)
  6. Seeded Dubai demo data
